💡 About

Buzzd is a free multiplayer trivia game. Jump into a random room with players from anywhere, or create a private room and challenge your friends. No sign-up needed.

❓ How to play

  1. Pick a random room, or create a private one and share the invite link.
  2. The host sets rounds, time, hints, difficulty and category.
  3. Everyone gets the same question. Tap the right answer before time runs out.

🏆 Scoring

Faster answers earn more points, and harder questions are worth more. Answer several in a row for a streak bonus. Hints remove wrong choices as the clock runs down, but waiting costs points.
